All constructive suggestions for improvement are very welcome indeed and please let me know what you would like to see in this page!

Group Activities

Each year the BSG organises sessions at the BCA Spring meeting, organises a 1 day winter meeting and organises a protein crystallography summer school.

BSG Winter Meeting 2026, Friday 11 December, University of Leicester.

Capturing Proteins in Motion: Structural Biology of Dynamic and Disordered Proteins. A one-day meeting at the Henry Wellcome Building, Leicester Institute of Structural and Chemical Biology, organised by Simon Newstead (Oxford) and Rachael Wilkinson (Leicester). Speakers: John Christodoulou (UCL), Rebecca Beveridge (Strathclyde), Sarah Rouse (Imperial), Cyril Dominguez (Leicester) and keynote Matthew Higgins (Oxford), with short talks selected from posters and poster prizes.

Registration is £80 (120 places) and closes on 26 November 2026; abstracts for short talks are due by 20 November 2026.
